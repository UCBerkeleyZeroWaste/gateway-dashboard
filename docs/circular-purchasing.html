Circular Purchasing

Check first. Refill first. Order last, and make it count.

A lot of waste is decided before it ever exists. Packaging, delivery trips, and duplicate orders are all locked in the moment something is bought.

90%+ Gateway's landfill diversion goal. Every order moves the building toward it or away from it.

The order of operations

  1. Reuse
  2. Refill
  3. Order reusables
  4. Compostables or EPP

You are here: steps 3 and 4. Steps 1 and 2, the ReUse Station and the Refill Hub, are on 02 Reuse Systems.

  • Order reusables. When supplies run out, order durable, reusable items from the Approved Products Catalog.
  • Compostables or EPP. Only when reuse isn't practical, choose approved compostable items or Environmentally Preferable Purchasing (EPP) products. Compostables must be paper- or fiber-based, never PLA.

Start with the Catalog

The Gateway Approved Products Catalog lists each item with its vendor and catalog number. It also tracks what's stocked at the ReUse Station, so check it before you buy anything at all.

When you need something single-use, the Catalog includes truly compostable items that Gateway's compost accepts.

The Science

Watch out for "compostable" plastic

PLA is a plastic made from plants. It only breaks down in high-heat industrial composting, which runs at around 58°C (136°F). Gateway's compost doesn't accept it, and because it looks like regular plastic, it contaminates the bins.

Source: Oh et al., "Structural evaluation of poly(lactic acid) degradation at standardized composting temperature of 58 degrees," Chemosphere, 2024

Catering and events

This is where good habits are easiest to drop. One delivery of disposable cups and trays can undo a department's careful ordering.

What a zero waste event looks like

Looks like this

  • Reusable serviceware first, like Dispatch Goods
  • Approved compostables only when needed
  • Bulk orders from approved vendors in the Catalog

Not this

  • Single-use plastic cups, plates, utensils, and trays filling the bin before the event is over

Purchasing standards we follow

Purchasing standards we follow

These are standard TRUE Zero Waste purchasing practices, and they're core to how the program works.

  • Durable, reusable goods over disposable ones
  • Used, refurbished, or remanufactured goods when available
  • Office paper with at least 30% post-consumer recycled content
  • Paper towels and tissue with at least 20% post-consumer recycled content
  • Sustainably produced paper and wood products
  • Approved vendors only
