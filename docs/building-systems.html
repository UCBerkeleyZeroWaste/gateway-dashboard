Building Systems

What goes in must come out.

We treat Gateway as one system with inputs and outputs, and track it like a mass balance. Everything that comes into the building eventually leaves it. Our job is to make sure as little as possible leaves as landfill.

How materials move through Gateway

Inputs come in, Gateway uses them, and every output has a place to go.

The Science

Diversion rate = (reuse + recycling + compost + recovery) ÷ everything discarded

TRUE certification requires a diversion rate of at least 90%, measured by weight over 12 months.

Source: TRUE Guide to Certification (GBCI, June 2023)

Behind the scenes and what you can do

Current Wins

  • Landfill, recycling, and compost are hauled separately, and each is tracked by weight.
  • Cardboard balers compress boxes for recycling.
  • Pallets are logged in and out so they can be reused or recycled.
  • Adding energy and water data alongside waste data. In progress

What you can do

Why no desk-side bins?

Gateway uses centralized waste stations instead. They improve sorting, reduce contamination, and cut down on plastic bin liners, in line with the campus-wide move away from desk-side collection.

Bring your waste to the stations. Not sure which bin? Check the Sorting Guide.
