Catering Policies

Better food. Less waste.

The basics

All catering at Gateway should prioritize reusable serviceware, minimize packaging, and cut unnecessary single-use items.

  • No single-use plastics or disposables by default.
  • Reusable trays, utensils, and serviceware come first.
  • Plan for proper waste sorting at every event.
  • Follow UC Berkeley zero waste standards.
See approved zero waste caterers

What to ask your caterer for

  • Food in bulk or tray format
  • No individually packaged meals, whenever possible
  • Reusable serviceware, such as Dispatch Goods
  • No single-use cutlery or packaging unless you specifically ask for it
  • Compostables only when reusables aren't feasible

Approved materials

Preferred

  • Reusable serviceware
  • Aluminum
  • Glass
  • Paper-based compostables
  • Fiber-based compostables

Not allowed

  • Plastic water bottles
  • Styrofoam
  • PLA plastics
  • #7 "compostable" plastics

Setup and cleanup

  • Set up clearly labeled compost, recycling, Dispatch, and landfill stations, using Gateway-approved signage.
  • Encourage guests to sort properly.
  • Return every Dispatch Goods container to a Dispatch return bin.
  • Coordinate with building resources for dishware setup and cleanup.
  • For larger events, coordinate with Cal Zero Waste.
