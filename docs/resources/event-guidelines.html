Event Guidelines

Great events. Zero waste.

Waste stations

Plan sustainability into the event from the start.

  • Every event needs compost, recycling, and landfill bins.
  • Use Gateway-approved signage at every station.

Planning a large event?

Partner with Cal Zero Waste. Student staff can provide sorting education, guidance for guests, and diversion support. Contact Sam Bunke.

Giveaways

Choose items people will actually use.

Recommended

  • Reusable notebooks
  • Ceramic, glass, or metal mugs
  • Phone chargers
  • Bamboo cutlery sets

Avoid

  • Non-refillable pens
  • Plastic pins
  • Vinyl stickers
  • Cheap plastic cups and water bottles
  • Flimsy keychains
  • Low-quality plastic tote or drawstring bags

Decor and signage

Recommended

  • Reusable banners
  • Reusable tabletop displays
  • Reusable tablecloths
  • Reusable plastic balloons
  • Dried flower petal or leaf confetti
  • Paper confetti
  • Bubbles in a plastic-free container

Avoid

  • Single-use decor
  • Disposable signage
  • Plastic-heavy decorations

Event checklist

  • Catering follows Gateway standards
  • Waste stations are planned
  • Signage is ready
  • Reusables come first
  • Giveaways meet the guidelines
  • Decor is reusable or compostable
