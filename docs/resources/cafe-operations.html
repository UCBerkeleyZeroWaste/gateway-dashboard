Café Operations

Small choices. Big impact.

Dispatch Goods

The Gateway café runs on a reuse-first model.

The café serves takeout in Dispatch Goods reusable containers instead of single-use ones. Borrow it, use it, return it. Dispatch cleans it, and it goes back into use.

  1. Finish your meal.
  2. Empty the food scraps. No need to wash or rinse.
  3. Return the container to an orange Dispatch return bin on campus.

Sort it right

Compost

  • Food scraps
  • Napkins
  • Compostable or wood serviceware

Recycle

  • #1 and #2 plastics
  • Glass bottles
  • Cans
  • Clean paper
  • Cardboard

Dispatch bin

  • Dispatch containers and lids
  • Broken Dispatch items

Landfill

  • Snack wrappers
  • #3 to #7 plastics
  • Styrofoam
  • Coffee lids and straws
