About the ReUse Station

How to get everything else

  • Stanley Hall stockroom

    Routine supplies. Buy in person at the Avantor Storeroom in Stanley Hall.

  • Department purchase

    Specialty items your department orders as needed, from Blaisdell's or another approved vendor. No unreviewed Amazon substitutes.

  • Building managed

    Shared items supplied for the building. Nothing to order.

Using the Stanley Hall stockroom
  • To set up a shopping account, email stanley.stockroom@avantorsciences.com.
  • In BearBuy, the Stanley Stockroom punchout catalog shows live stock and current pricing, so check before you go.
  • For stock alerts, discount rates, and inventory updates, see News and Announcements on BearBuy's Research/Lab Supplies & Support Guided Dashboard.
  • A partnership to sell Blaisdell's office supplies through the stockroom is in progress.
